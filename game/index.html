Spin Doctor

Same data, different story. Can you make a neighborhood look safe, or dangerous, without changing a single number?
Walk to a case file and press E
Real Charlotte-Mecklenburg Police Department report counts, aggregated by Neighborhood Profile Area (NPA), 2017–2025. NPAs do not match everyday neighborhood boundaries. This game shows how numbers can be framed; it does not say any neighborhood is safe or dangerous, and reported counts are not a measure of all crime.
